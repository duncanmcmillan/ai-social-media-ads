The Placements & Audience panel has just two toggles, but they have a big impact on how Meta spends your budget. Both relate to Meta's Advantage+ system — their AI-driven optimisation layer.

The first toggle is Advantage+ Placements.

When this is on, Meta automatically decides where to show your ads — Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels, Audience Network, Messenger, and so on. Meta's algorithm shifts spend toward wherever it's getting the best results in real time.

The alternative is Manual placements, where you specify exactly which surfaces you want. That gives you more control but requires more active management.

For most advertisers, especially when you're starting out or scaling, Advantage+ Placements is the recommended approach. Meta has significantly more data than any individual advertiser about which placements work best for a given objective.

The second toggle is Advantage+ Audience.

When this is on, you still set your base targeting — countries, age range, interests — but Meta is allowed to expand beyond that if it finds better-performing people outside your defined audience. Think of it as a signal rather than a hard limit.

When it's off, Meta sticks strictly to the audience you've defined. This is useful when you have a very specific audience in mind, or if you're running retargeting where you want precise control over who sees the ad.

These two settings become the default for every new ad set you create. You can still override them on a per-ad-set basis in the New Campaign wizard if you need different behaviour for a specific campaign.
