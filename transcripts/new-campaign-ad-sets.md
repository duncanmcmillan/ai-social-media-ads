# New Campaign — Ad Sets Step — Screencast Transcript

**Panel:** New Campaign › Step 2: Ad Sets
**Purpose:** Define one or more ad sets — each with its own audience, placement, optimisation, and schedule.

---

## Intro

Step two is Ad Sets. An ad set sits between the campaign and the individual ads — it's where you define who you're targeting, where your ads will appear, and when they run.

You can have multiple ad sets in one campaign, which is useful if you want to test different audiences or placements simultaneously. Each ad set gets its own tab.

---

## Adding Ad Sets

If you used the AI draft builder on step one, your ad sets will already be here. If not, the panel starts empty with an **Add ad set** button.

Click it to create your first ad set. You'll see a tab appear at the top. You can add more tabs to create additional ad sets, and remove them with the × button on the tab. The campaign-level budget will be distributed across all active ad sets if you chose Campaign Budget on step one.

---

## Ad Set Name

The first field is the **Ad Set Name**. Give it a name that reflects the audience or test — for example "UK 25–44 Prospecting" or "Retargeting 30 days". This makes it much easier to read reports later.

---

## Audience

The **Audience** section has three fields:

- **Target Countries**: enter the ISO country codes, comma-separated. These will be pre-filled from your workspace defaults.
- **Min Age** and **Max Age**: the age range for this ad set, again pre-filled from your defaults.

If you're running multiple ad sets to test different audiences, this is where you differentiate them — for example one ad set for UK only, another for UK + Ireland.

---

## Placements & Optimisation

The **Placements** toggle lets you choose between **Advantage+** and **Manual**.

With Advantage+ (recommended), Meta decides which placements to use. With Manual, you'd specify in Ads Manager after launch — the app currently sets manual placement as a flag; you define the exact surfaces in Meta Ads Manager.

Below that are two dropdowns:

- **Optimisation Goal**: what Meta bids toward. For a Sales campaign, you'd typically choose OFFSITE_CONVERSIONS or VALUE. For Traffic, LINK_CLICKS. This should align with your campaign objective.
- **Billing Event**: when you get charged. IMPRESSIONS is standard for most campaigns — you pay per thousand impressions and Meta handles bid strategy.

---

## Budget & Schedule

The final section is **Budget & Schedule**.

Choose between **Run continuously** — the ad set runs indefinitely until you pause it — or **Set start & end**, which lets you enter specific dates. If you choose scheduled, two date pickers appear for start date and end date.

Note that if you chose Campaign Budget on step one, you won't set a budget here — the campaign-level budget covers all ad sets. If you chose Ad Set Budget, you'd set the amount per ad set in Meta Ads Manager after launch (this field is managed by Meta at the ad set level).

---

## Multiple Ad Sets

If you're testing multiple audiences, click the **+ Add** button in the tab bar to create another ad set. Each tab is independent — you can configure a completely different audience, placement, and schedule for each one.

The summary panel on the right will show the total number of ad sets as you add them.

---

## Wrap-up

When all your ad sets are configured, click **Next: Creatives** to move to the final step where you'll upload your images or videos and write the ad copy.
