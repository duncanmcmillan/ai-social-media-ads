The New Campaign tab is a three-step wizard. Step one covers the campaign itself — the name, the objective Meta will optimise for, and the budget. On the right you'll see a summary panel that updates as you fill things in.

At the very top there's the Build with AI bar. This is optional but worth trying — type a sentence describing your business or what you're trying to achieve, like "Summer sale for a UK-based outdoor clothing brand targeting walkers and climbers", and click Build draft.

Claude will generate a campaign name, select a relevant objective, and pre-fill the ad set structure. You can then edit everything it produces. It's a starting point, not a finished product.

The first field is Campaign Name. This is how the campaign will appear in Meta Ads Manager, so name it something that makes sense to you — typically the product, promotion, and date.

The Objective section has six options shown as pill buttons.

Sales — optimise for purchases. This is the most common choice for e-commerce.

Traffic — drive clicks to your website.

Leads — collect lead form submissions or website leads.

Engagement — get more video views, messages, or page follows.

Awareness — maximise reach at the lowest cost per person.

App Promotion — drive installs or in-app events.

Click the one that matches your goal. A hint below the buttons explains what Meta will optimise for. Choose carefully — the objective drives the entire optimisation strategy for the campaign.

The Budget section has two parts.

First, choose between Campaign budget and Ad set budget.

Campaign budget, formerly Campaign Budget Optimisation or CBO: Meta controls how spend is distributed across your ad sets, shifting money to wherever it's getting the best results. This is generally recommended.

Ad set budget: you allocate a fixed budget to each ad set yourself. More control, more manual work.

Then choose Daily or Lifetime and enter the amount. Daily budgets run continuously day by day. Lifetime budgets spread across a fixed end date.

The URL Tracking field is for UTM parameters. If you've already set a UTM template in the Workspace Enhancements panel, that value will be pre-filled here. You can override it for this specific campaign if needed.

The last toggle is Activate Immediately.

When this is off — which is the default — every campaign, ad set, and ad is created in Facebook in a Paused state. Nothing spends. You'll need to go into Meta Ads Manager to review everything and turn it on manually.

When it's on, the campaign goes live the moment you click Launch. Only turn this on if you've already checked all your settings and are confident in the setup.

On the right, the summary panel shows a setup score — a zero to four indicator that turns orange dots as you complete each section. Below that, campaign progress shows three checkboxes for campaign details, ad sets, and creatives. The At a Glance section gives a live summary of campaign name, objective, budget, and counts. And How it Works gives a quick reminder of what each step does and how creatives multiplied by ad sets equals total ads.

Once you're happy with the campaign settings, click Next: Ad Sets at the bottom to move on to step two.
