# Meta Defaults — Screencast Transcript

**Panel:** Workspace › Meta Defaults
**Purpose:** Set the core Meta settings that apply to every new campaign by default.

---

## Intro

The Meta Defaults panel is where you set four things that every Facebook campaign needs: your Facebook Page, your Instagram account preference, your Meta Pixel, and your Website URL.

These values are saved as workspace defaults, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you create a new campaign. You can always override them at the campaign level if needed.

---

## Facebook Page

The first field is **Facebook Page**. This is required — it's the identity that appears on your ads, the "from" name and profile picture that people see in the Facebook feed.

If you're connected to Facebook, the app will load your pages automatically and show them in a dropdown. Just select the right page. If you haven't connected yet, you can type the Page ID directly.

If your pages don't appear, click **Refresh pages** and the app will re-fetch them.

---

## Instagram Account

Below that is the **Use Facebook Page for Instagram** toggle.

If you have a standalone Instagram account linked to your Business Manager, you don't need this — the app will use that by default. But if you're running Instagram ads without a linked Instagram account, turn this toggle on. It tells Meta to use your Facebook Page as the Instagram identity, which is a supported approach.

---

## Meta Pixel

Next is the **Meta Pixel** field. This is required for conversion tracking — without it, Meta can't optimise for purchases, leads, or any website events.

If you're connected and have an ad account selected, the app will load your pixels and show them in a dropdown. Select the right one.

If your pixel isn't in the list, click **Refresh pixels**. If you need to enter the ID manually, you can type it directly into the input field.

---

## Website URL

The **Website URL** field is your default landing page — the destination people go to when they click your ad.

Enter the full URL including `https://`. This is also used by the AI copy generator on the Creatives step to understand your business context.

---

## Exclude Custom Audiences

The last toggle is **Exclude Custom Audiences**. When this is on, the app will apply your saved exclusion audiences to every ad set, preventing you from showing ads to people who've already converted. This is good practice for prospecting campaigns.

---

## Wrap-up

These four settings — Page, Pixel, Website URL, and Instagram preference — are the minimum required to publish a campaign. If any of them are missing, you'll see a warning on the New Campaign screen reminding you to come back and fill them in.
