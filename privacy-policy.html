Privacy Policy

AI Social Media Ads  ·  Last updated: August 2026

AI Social Media Ads ("the App", "we", "us") is a desktop application that helps marketing professionals create and manage Facebook advertising campaigns. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Access

When you connect your Meta account, the App requests the following permissions on your behalf:

  • ads_management — to create, read, update, and pause campaigns, ad sets, and ads in your ad account.
  • ads_read — to read performance insights for your campaigns and creatives.
  • business_management — to access your Business Manager ad accounts.
  • pages_read_engagement — to list Facebook Pages you administer so you can select one as the ad identity.

We only request the minimum permissions required to provide the App's core functionality. We do not request access to your personal profile, friends list, posts, messages, or any data unrelated to advertising.

2. How We Use Your Data

All data accessed through the Meta Marketing API is used solely to provide the App's features to you. Specifically:

  • Your access token is used to authenticate API calls to the Meta Graph API on your behalf.
  • Ad account, campaign, ad set, and ad data is displayed within the App to allow you to manage your campaigns.
  • Performance insights (impressions, clicks, spend, etc.) are fetched and displayed in the Monitoring and Optimisation tabs.
  • Facebook Pages and Pixels are listed so you can select them when creating campaigns.

We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your data or Meta account information to any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

The App is a local desktop application. All data is stored on your device only:

  • Your Meta access token and app credentials are stored in your operating system's encrypted secret storage (Electron safeStorage), not in plain text.
  • Workspace settings (targeting defaults, naming templates, etc.) are stored in your browser's localStorage on your local machine.
  • No data is transmitted to any server operated by AI Social Media Ads. All API calls go directly from your device to Meta's Graph API.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete all stored credentials and settings at any time by using the Delete All Data option in the App's Workspace settings. This removes your access token, app credentials, and all locally stored settings from your device.

Revoking the App's access via your Facebook App Settings will immediately invalidate the stored access token.

5. Third-Party Services

The App communicates directly with the following third-party APIs:

  • Meta Graph API (graph.facebook.com) — for all Facebook advertising operations. Meta's privacy practices are governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic Claude API (api.anthropic.com) — optionally used to generate ad copy suggestions. If you provide an Anthropic API key, ad creative context (tone, hook, website URL) is sent to Anthropic to generate copy. No personal data or ad account identifiers are included in these requests. Anthropic's privacy practices are governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by marketing professionals and businesses. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
